## Common Information

Typical GraphQL services provide access to two types of requests: Queries and Mutations. The last can include more complicated structures in the request, like Arguments, Aliases, Fragments, Directives, and Variables. Queries are used to fetch the data, where Mutations are needed in order to modify server-side data. For more information, please follow Queries and Mutations section at [graphql.org](https://graphql.org).
